Tithes & Offerings
Giving is an act of worship and a response of gratitude to God for all that He has done in our lives
It reflects our faith, obedience, and trust in His provision.

Tithe

Tithe is the practice of giving a portion of our income to the Lord, acknowledging Him as our source and provider.

The Bible teaches that when we honor God through giving, He blesses and sustains us according to His promises.

Through tithing, we support the work of God’s kingdom and participate in spreading His Word.

Offering

Offering is giving beyond the tithe, led by a willing and generous heart.

Offerings help extend ministry, support those in need, and spread the Gospel.

We are encouraged to give cheerfully and trust God in all things.

Why We Give

  • Why We Give
  • To honor God
  • To support His work
  • To serve others
  • To grow in faith
