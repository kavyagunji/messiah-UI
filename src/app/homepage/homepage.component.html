At Messiah Fellowship, our desire is to see lives transformed through the saving knowledge of Jesus Christ. We are committed to loving God, loving people, and building strong families rooted in faith and truth.

We are a welcoming community where prayer is central, the Word of God is honored, and the Holy Spirit leads. Our heart is to nurture spiritual growth and raise disciples who reflect Christ in their homes, workplaces, and society.

As a church family, we believe every person matters and every life has purpose in God.

Pastor Sajja Eliazr

Founder & President, Messiah Fellowship

Pastor Sajja Eliazer is the founder and president of Messiah Fellowship, a ministry that has faithfully served for more than five decades. Born as Sajja Gowthameswara Rao and raised in a Hindu family in the village of Koduru, Mudinepalli Mandalam, his life took a transformative turn after his younger son, Ratna Kumar, was miraculously healed from a life-threatening illness. Through this powerful encounter, he came to know Jesus Christ personally and surrendered his life to the Gospel.

For over 50 years, Pastor Eliazer has been in full-time ministry, passionately proclaiming the saving and miracle-working power of Jesus Christ across Andhra Pradesh and beyond. Known for his bold preaching and unwavering faith, he has become a respected and influential voice in Christian ministry throughout the region.

His television program, Mahima Gadiyalu, is among the most-watched Christian broadcasts, reaching countless homes and sharing the message of hope, healing, and salvation.

Through his leadership, Messiah Fellowship has grown into a thriving community of believers in Vijayawada. The Messiah Convention Center, located at 100 Feet Road, Autonagar Gate, stands as a testimony to God’s faithfulness and serves as a place where the Word of God continues to be proclaimed.

Pastor Eliazer and his wife, Mrs. Kamala, have been blessed with two sons and a daughter, as well as eight grandchildren. Their family remains deeply rooted in faith and ministry. We warmly invite you to visit and experience the presence of God with us. You are just one step away from victory.

Messiah Bible College
Equipping Leaders. Strengthening the Church

Messiah Bible College exists to equip believers with a deeper understanding of God’s Word and to prepare faithful leaders for ministry. Through structured biblical teaching, spiritual formation, and practical ministry training, we aim to raise men and women who are rooted in Scripture and empowered by the Holy Spirit.

Our heart is to train disciples who will serve the Church with integrity, wisdom, and a strong foundation in Christian doctrine. Whether called to pastoral ministry, missions, teaching, or church leadership, students are nurtured to grow both academically and spiritually.
