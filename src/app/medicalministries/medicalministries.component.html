Free Medical Camp

At Messiah Fellowship, we believe that caring for people includes both spiritual and physical well-being. To support the community, we organize free medical camps every three months, creating access to essential healthcare for those who may not be able to afford it.

These camps are open to everyone, irrespective of religion, background, or social status, reflecting our commitment to serve with love, equality, and compassion

Our Mission

Our goal is to bring quality healthcare closer to the community by providing timely medical support, early diagnosis, and care through qualified professionals.

We aim to ensure that no one is deprived of basic healthcare due to financial limitations

What We Provide
  • Free doctor consultations by experienced medical professionals
  • Free medical lab tests for diagnosis and screening
  • Free medicines based on prescriptions
  • Basic health check-ups and guidance
  • Support and care in a welcoming environment
How It Helps

Through these regular camps, many individuals and families receive much-needed medical attention and health awareness

  • Early detection of health issues
  • Access to professional medical advice
  • Relief from the financial burden of healthcare
  • Improved health awareness in the community

Our Commitment

We are committed to continuing these medical camps regularly, reaching more people and making healthcare accessible to all. Every camp is an opportunity to serve, care, and make a meaningful difference in people’s lives

Carry each other’s burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ

A Message of Compassion

We believe that serving others in their time of need is a reflection of God’s love. Through these medical camps, we extend not just treatment, but care, respect, and hope to every individual who walks in.
