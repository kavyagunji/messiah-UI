Widow Care Ministry

At Messiah Fellowship, we are committed to caring for widows with compassion, honor, and practical support. Many widows face loneliness, financial hardship, and lack of care, but through this ministry, we strive to bring hope and dignity into their lives.

We regularly provide essential support such as food, clothing, and fellowship gatherings, ensuring that no one feels forgotten. Beyond physical needs, we also offer spiritual encouragement, prayer, and a loving community where they are valued and uplifted.

This ministry reflects the heart of God to stand with the vulnerable, to comfort the broken, and to remind every widow that she is deeply loved and not alone.

A father to the fatherless, a defender of widows, is God in His holy dwelling - Psalm 68:5
