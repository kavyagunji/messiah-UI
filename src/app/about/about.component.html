ABOUT US
A CHRIST-CENTERED CHURCH IN VIJAYAWADA

Christ-Centered, Spirit-Led and
Community-Driven.

Messiah Fellowship is a Christ-centered church committed to proclaiming the truth of God's Word and nurturing believers in their spiritual journey.

Founded with a vision to bring people into a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ, the church emphasizes sound doctrine, faithful discipleship, and a life led by the Holy Spirit.

At Messiah Fellowship, we believe in building a strong, loving community where lives are transformed, hope is restored, and Christ is glorified.

Building lives through Christ
and strengthening faith together

Mission

To lead people into the saving knowledge of Jesus Christ, nurturing hearts, families, and lives through the truth of God’s Word.

Vision

To help people discover their identity in Christ, walk in God’s purpose, and grow into mature disciples filled with faith, wisdom, and the Holy Spirit.

What We Value

Love

Love is the foundation of our faith and the greatest commandment, guiding how we live and serve others.

Excellence

We pursue excellence in all we do, honoring God and inspiring those around us.

Spiritual Growth

We encourage growth through prayer, the Word of God, and the leading of the Holy Spirit.

Teaching & Discipleship

We believe that sound teaching and intentional discipleship bring true transformation in people's lives.

Family & Community

We value families as God's design and build a strong, supportive church community.

Restoration & Hope

We believe God restores and rebuilds lives, bringing hope, healing, and purpose to those in need.

What We Believe

Our beliefs are firmly grounded in the Holy Bible and centered on the life and teachings of Jesus Christ.

The Scriptures

2 Timothy 3:16; 2 Peter 1:21

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit co equal and eternal. Jesus Christ is the Word made flesh, and the Holy Spirit proceeds from the Father and the Son.

The Godhead

John 1:1,14; John 14:26 John 15:26

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit co equal and eternal. Jesus Christ is the Word made flesh, and the Holy Spirit proceeds from the Father and the Son.

Man, Fall & Redemption

Romans 3:23; John 3:16 Ephesians 2:8–10

We believe man was created in God’s image but fell into sin through disobedience. Salvation is made possible through Jesus Christ, who redeemed humanity by His sacrifice It is received by grace through faith, not by works.

Eternal Life & New Birth

John 3:3–5; John 5:24

We believe that repentance is essential for salvation, and the new birth is necessary for all. Through Christ, believers receive eternal life.

Water Baptism

Matthew 28:19; Romans 6:4

We believe in water baptism by immersion for believers, as commanded by the Lord. It is a public declaration of faith and identification with Christ’s death, burial, and resurrection.

The Holy Spirit

John 14:16; Acts 1:8

We believe the Holy Spirit is the third person of the Trinity, who dwells within believers as a helper and comforter. He empowers believers and manifests through spiritual gifts.

Sanctification

Hebrews 12:14; 1 Thessalonians 5:23

We believe sanctification is both a definite and progressive work of grace, beginning at salvation and continuing until Christ’s return.

Divine Healing

Mark 16:18; James 5:14–15

We believe divine healing is provided through the atonement of Christ and is available through prayer and faith.

Resurrection & Return of Christ

Matthew 28:19; Romans 6:4

We believe in the resurrection of the dead and the second coming of Jesus Christ. He will return as King of Kings, and believers will be gathered to Him.

Eternal Judgment

Revelation 20:15; Hebrews 9:27

We believe in eternal life for those who accept Christ and eternal separation from God for those who reject Him.
