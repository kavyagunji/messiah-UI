Flood Relief Outreach

Vijayawada has faced recurring floods due to heavy rains and rising water levels in the Krishna River. During such times many families especially those in low lying areas, are severely affected. Homes are submerged, daily life is disrupted, and access to food, clean water, and basic necessities becomes a major challenge.

In these difficult moments, communities look for hope, support, and immediate relief.

Our Response

Messiah Fellowship stepped forward with a heart to serve and support those affected by the floods. With the help of dedicated volunteers and members, we reached out to impacted areas to provide essential relief and encouragement.

Our mission was simple: to extend God’s love through practical help and compassionate care.

What We Provide
  • Cooked food packets for families in need
  • Drinking water from bottles for safe consumption
  • Blankets to protect from cold and damp conditions
  • Essential ration kits for daily survival
  • On-ground support through volunteers in affected areas
How It Helps

Through this outreach, we provided timely support and relief to many families facing hardship.

  • Hundreds of families supported
  • Essential supplies distributed across affected communities
  • Immediate relief is provided during critical conditions
  • Hope and encouragement are shared with those in distress

A Message of Hope

In times of crisis, even a small act of kindness can make a big difference. We believe that serving others is a reflection of God’s love in action. Through this outreach, we not only met physical needs but also stood alongside people with care, prayer, and compassion

Whoever is kind to the poor lends to the Lord, and He will reward them for what they have done.
