Children Ministry/Sunday School

Sunday, 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM

The Children’s Ministry at Messiah Fellowship is dedicated to nurturing young hearts in the love and truth of God’s Word.

We provide a joyful and engaging environment where children can learn Bible stories, grow in faith, and develop a personal relationship with Jesus Christ. Through interactive teaching, songs, and activities, children are encouraged to understand and apply biblical principles in their daily lives.

Our goal is to help children build a strong spiritual foundation from an early age, guiding them to grow in character, obedience, and faith. We believe that teaching children in a loving and supportive environment helps them become confident and Christ-centered individuals.

Through fun-filled sessions and meaningful lessons, children are equipped to praise, serve, and follow the Lord, while enjoying fellowship with other children.
