Kamala Arts & Craft
Training Center

The Kamala Arts & Craft Training Center, an initiative of Messiah Fellowship, is dedicated to empowering women with practical skills that help them build a better future. Through structured training programs, participants are equipped with tailoring and stitching skills that enable them to earn a livelihood with dignity and confidence.

Our Mission

The center welcomes individuals from all backgrounds, irrespective of religion or community. It is open to everyone who is willing to learn and grow, reflecting the heart of Messiah Fellowship to serve society with love, equality, and compassion.

What We Provide
  • Free tailoring and stitching training
  • Hands-on practical learning sessions
  • Sewing machine support for selected participants
  • Skill development for self-employment
  • Open access for all communities
How It Helps

Upon successful completion, many participants are supported with sewing machines, enabling them to start their own work from home or secure employment opportunities. What begins as learning soon becomes a pathway to financial independence and self-reliance.

Transforming skills into livelihoods and opportunities into lasting change.
